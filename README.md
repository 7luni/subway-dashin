# Subway Dashin
Subway Dashin mixes the endless running of Subway Surfers with the tough platforming and rhythm play of Geometry Dash. Avoid obstacles, leap over dangers, face friends, and test your skills!

Built entirely by me and Claude AI.

Features

8 Unique Levels – Explore eight levels with many obstacles and different play styles.

Duels – Face other players and see who wins.

Death Link – Share your fate. When one player dies, the other player dies too.

Community Levels – Find and play levels made by the community.

No Coins Mode – Play without coins and focus on staying alive.

Challenging Gameplay – Test your reflexes, timing, and accuracy.

More Features – Many more items and surprises to find!
